## 3. Third-party material

Third-party dependencies, libraries, fonts, icons, images, datasets, code, and other externally sourced material are **not** automatically owned by ONYX.

Each such item must remain subject to its applicable license or permission. A separate dependency and asset licensing inventory will be established in the licensing-compliance phase.

## 4. Contributor and contractor evidence

The following evidence should be collected and retained before commercialization where applicable:

- Founder-created work and authorship records
- Employee intellectual-property agreements
- Contractor intellectual-property assignments
- Confidentiality agreements
- External contributor agreements
- Commissioned design and branding agreements
- Third-party asset licenses and permissions

The absence of a record in this register does not itself establish that an ownership right does or does not exist; it identifies evidence that should be collected or verified.
