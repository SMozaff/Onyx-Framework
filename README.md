# ONYX
### One connected workspace for the way your organization works.

**Bring people, work, and organizational activity together in one clear experience.**

ONYX is an organization-focused platform built around a simple idea: teams work better when their everyday activities, responsibilities, and decisions feel connected. From organizing work to coordinating people and managing the structure of an organization, ONYX aims to make complex operations easier to understand and easier to move forward.

[Explore ONYX on GitHub](https://github.com/SMozaff/Onyx-Framework)

---

## Work with greater clarity

Organizations rarely struggle because they lack things to do. They struggle when priorities, responsibilities, and communication become scattered.

ONYX is being developed to give teams a more coherent place to organize activity, understand ownership, and keep work moving—without losing sight of the people and structure behind it.

### A clearer view of work
Keep tasks, responsibilities, and progress connected so teams can spend less time chasing context and more time making progress.

### People and organizations, together
Support a structured way to represent organizations, teams, and administrative responsibilities as the organization grows.

### Coordination that feels connected
Bring day-to-day collaboration and organizational activity into a shared experience, helping people stay aligned around what matters.

### Designed for more than one screen
ONYX is being shaped for a multi-device experience, so organizational work can fit more naturally into the way people use their computers and mobile devices.

---

## One platform. Many everyday needs.

ONYX brings together a growing set of organizational capabilities, with a focus on making them feel like parts of one experience.

- **Work management** — organize tasks, responsibilities, and follow-through.
- **Team coordination** — support communication and shared awareness across ongoing work.
- **Organization management** — represent organizational structure and administrative roles.
- **Operational workflows** — help teams coordinate activities that involve people, decisions, and accountability.
- **A connected experience** — bring these capabilities together across the ONYX product family.

The experience is evolving as the project develops. The goal is not to add complexity; it is to make organizational work feel more understandable, connected, and manageable.

---

## Built around the people doing the work

ONYX is intended for teams and organizations that want a clearer way to coordinate everyday operations, including:

- Growing organizations that need more structure as their teams expand.
- Administrators who coordinate people, access, and organizational setup.
- Teams managing multiple responsibilities and ongoing activities.
- Projects where collaboration and accountability need to work together.

Whether the challenge is keeping work visible, coordinating responsibilities, or bringing organizational activity into one place, ONYX is being built with practical day-to-day use in mind.

---

## Our direction

We believe useful organizational software should make work feel simpler—not make people learn another maze of processes.

ONYX is moving toward an experience that is:

- **Clear** — make responsibilities and activity easier to understand.
- **Connected** — reduce the distance between people, work, and decisions.
- **Adaptable** — support different organizational needs and ways of working.
- **Consistent** — provide a coherent experience across the ONYX product family.
- **Practical** — focus on real operational needs rather than complexity for its own sake.

These principles guide the product as it continues to take shape.
